== REQUIRED SECTIONS ==

SECTION 1 — YOUR RIGHT TO CANCEL
Under Israeli Consumer Protection Law, customers who purchased online
have the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving the product.
For services: within 14 days of contract, or before service begins.
State this right clearly and prominently at the top.

SECTION 2 — DIGITAL PRODUCTS (include only if DIGITAL_DOWNLOADS = YES)
Once a digital product has been downloaded or accessed, the 14-day
cancellation right MAY be waived ONLY if:
a) The customer was informed of this BEFORE purchase
b) The customer gave EXPLICIT consent via checkbox (unchecked by default):
'I understand that once downloaded I waive my right to cancel'
Include this checkbox language in the output.

SECTION 3 — FREELANCE / SERVICE WORK (include only if FREELANCE = YES)
If service has begun with customer consent: proportional payment only.
State the calculation method (hourly rate or milestone %).
If service NOT begun: full refund within 14 days.

SECTION 4 — REFUND PROCESS
How to request: email [CONTACT_EMAIL] subject: 'Cancellation Request'
Include: order number, purchase date, reason (optional)
Timeline: within 14 business days
Method: original payment method only

SECTION 5 — NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS (must be specific, not a blanket clause)
Customized/personalized items made specifically for the client
Digital products accessed/downloaded (with proper prior consent)
Completed services already delivered and accepted by client
Third-party fees already paid (domain, hosting, stock photos)

SECTION 6 — GOVERNING LAW
Governed by Israeli Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981.
Contact [CONTACT_EMAIL] before initiating any dispute.
