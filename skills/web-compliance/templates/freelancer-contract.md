# 📜 Freelancer Contract + Handoff Checklist

Build a PROFESSIONAL FREELANCE WEB DEVELOPMENT CONTRACT in [LANGUAGE]
(with English translation alongside) for Israeli clients.
This contract must comply with Israeli contract law, including
Amendment 3 to the Contracts (General Part) Law (January 2025).

== PROJECT DETAILS ==
Project description: [PROJECT_DESCRIPTION]
Estimated delivery: [DELIVERY_WEEKS] weeks from contract signing
Number of revision rounds included: [REVISION_ROUNDS]
What "revision" means: changes within original scope, NOT new features

== THE 12 CONTRACT CLAUSES ==

CLAUSE 1 — SCOPE OF WORK
Exact list of deliverables (be specific):
- [PROJECT_DESCRIPTION]
- Cookie banner compliant with Amendment 13
- Accessibility widget (IS 5568)
- Privacy Policy page
- Terms of Use page
- Accessibility Statement page

NOT included (out of scope):
- SEO optimization
- Content writing
- Logo or branding design
- Server hosting setup
- Ongoing maintenance after delivery

CLAUSE 2 — PAYMENT TERMS
Total fee: ₪[TOTAL_FEE] + VAT (if applicable)
Payment structure:
  50% deposit upon signing — work begins after payment received
  50% final payment upon delivery — before files/access transferred
Payment method: [PAYMENT_METHOD]
Late payment: If final payment not received within 7 days of delivery,
contractor may withhold file transfer and charge 5% monthly interest
under the Adjudication of Interest and Linkage Law, 5721-1961

CLAUSE 3 — TIMELINE
Start date: [START_DATE]
Delivery date: within [DELIVERY_WEEKS] weeks of start
Client feedback window: Client must provide feedback within 5 business
days of receiving drafts. Delay by client extends deadline accordingly.
Contractor may pause work if client is unresponsive for 14+ days.

CLAUSE 4 — REVISIONS
Included: [REVISION_ROUNDS] rounds of revisions within original scope
Additional revisions: ₪[HOURLY_RATE]/hour
Revision defined: Changes to existing elements. New pages, features, or
major design changes are NOT revisions — they require a new quote.

CLAUSE 5 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon receipt of final payment, client owns all deliverables.
Contractor retains right to display project in portfolio unless client
requests otherwise in writing.
Third-party licenses (fonts, stock photos, plugins): client is responsible
for obtaining and maintaining required licenses.

CLAUSE 6 — CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES
Client must provide:
- All content (text, images, logo files in high resolution)
- Access to hosting/domain if contractor is to deploy
- Timely feedback within agreed windows
Delay in providing content extends the delivery date accordingly.

CLAUSE 7 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Contractor's maximum liability is limited to the total project fee paid.
Contractor is NOT liable for:
- Business losses resulting from website downtime
- Third-party service failures (Google, hosting, domain registrar)
- Losses due to client's failure to keep legal pages updated
- Client use of the website in violation of applicable law
- SEO performance or business results

CLAUSE 8 — LEGAL COMPLIANCE DISCLAIMER
"Contractor provides legally-structured template pages
(Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Accessibility Statement)
as part of this project. These are technical templates only.
Client is solely responsible for:
- Reviewing all legal pages with a qualified Israeli attorney
- Keeping legal page content accurate and up to date
- Ensuring ongoing compliance with Amendment 13, IS 5568,
  and all other applicable Israeli laws
Contractor is not liable for legal claims arising from the content
of these pages after delivery."

CLAUSE 9 — WARRANTIES
Contractor warrants that delivered work will function as described
for 30 days after delivery.
Warranty covers: bugs in delivered functionality.
After warranty period: additional work is billable at ₪[HOURLY_RATE]/hour.

CLAUSE 10 — TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice.
If client terminates: client pays for all work completed to termination
date, calculated at ₪[HOURLY_RATE]/hour, deducted from deposit.
If contractor terminates due to client breach: deposit is non-refundable.

CLAUSE 11 — CONFIDENTIALITY
Contractor will keep client's business information confidential.
Client will keep contractor's methods and pricing confidential.
This clause survives termination of the contract.

CLAUSE 12 — GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This contract is governed by the laws of the State of Israel.
In accordance with Israeli Contract Law Amendment 3 (January 2025),
this contract shall be interpreted according to its plain written
meaning. Disputes shall be resolved exclusively in the courts of
[YOUR_CITY], Israel.
Alternative: disputes under ₪75,000 may be filed in Small Claims Court.
