What do you get?

01 / SKILL

Instructions for your agent

A standalone skill with 13 prompt templates and six source packs. It prepares scoped implementation instructions from your project facts; loading it does not install code.

Design in your browser

A local studio with 12 themes, four banner languages, and exports for WordPress or custom websites. Use it without an account or an AI assistant.

Three composed prompt examples, fictional project profiles and a worksheet for consent, storage, accessibility and removal. Record the actual results on your customer's site.

YOUR FIRST RUN

Give it a concrete task.

Reopen the project in your agent, confirm the skill is discovered, and use this request. Replace bracketed details with real facts. The skill should ask or mark what is missing.

Use the web-compliance skill to prepare a cookie-banner implementation prompt.
Target markets: [actual countries and US states / Canadian provinces].
Output language: [English / Hebrew / Arabic / Russian].
Website/framework: [facts]. Privacy URL: [URL].
Tracking tools: [actual tools, or unknown].
List missing facts and source checks. Do not change or publish my site.

Know the scope before you use it.

  • The banner communicates consent. Connect and test the real tracking tools; scripts from other plugins are not automatically blocked.
  • The document generator produces Hebrew drafts for Israel only. Changing interface or prompt language does not expand legal coverage.
  • All source packs still require legal review. The kit does not automatically scan a website or provide compliance or accessibility certification.
  • The generated plugin is a banner component. The separate Website Compliance Launch Review reporting skill is not part of this kit.
