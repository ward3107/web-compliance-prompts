Before relying on documents or your website’s legal implementation, consult a lawyer experienced in the topics and jurisdictions relevant to your business.

The toolkit currently has no partner lawyer. Share the packet with a lawyer you choose. Partner details, scope, prices and turnaround times will appear only once agreed.

What goes in the packet?

  • Business details, target markets and questionnaire answers.
  • Unanswered questions and details that need completing.
  • Available source packs, with their review status.
  • Suggested discussion topics: privacy, terms, accessibility, tracking and other issues based on your answers.

What should you ask them to review?

Agree the scope, applicable jurisdictions, documents, website checks, price and turnaround time. Any opinion or signed document should identify the version and scope reviewed.

Preparing a packet does not send it to anyone. You download it and choose whom to share it with. Completion of the review is not recorded automatically on this website.
